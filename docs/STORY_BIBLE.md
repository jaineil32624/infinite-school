# Infinite School — Story Bible

> Spoilers for every chapter. This is the source of truth for writers.

## Premise

Halloway Academy was built in 1921 over the **Stillwell**, a perfectly round spring whose water "could keep a flower fresh for a year." The spring is not water. It is something old that *keeps* things, and it loves the school sitting on top of it.

On **Thursday, October 13, 1977**, the town voted to close Halloway the next day. During an afternoon fire drill, fourteen-year-old **Felix Bram** hid in the 2-F supply closet to prank his teacher, **Aldous Grey**. Grey counted twenty-one students instead of twenty-two and closed the door. The bell rang. The Stillwell, terrified of being paved over, *kept the day*. It has been Thursday ever since. The town outside has quietly moved on: the closure has been "postponed" every few years, new students cycle in, and each one lives the same Thursday until they drift away. Most never notice.

The player is the first person in almost fifty years who carries memories across midnight. In the Stillwell's ledger, their name is marked **"Pending."**

**Core theme:** the cost of refusing to say goodbye. Every major character is keeping something, and the game asks whether keeping it is love or fear.

## The five phases

| Phase | Name | Player experience | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal School Life | Classes, friends, odd little details | Ch. 1 |
| 2 | Strange Events | The day repeats, Theo vanishes, room 2-F appears | Ch. 1 |
| 3 | Conspiracy | The Bellwether Society, the archive, the Stillwell | Ch. 1 ends here, Ch. 2 |
| 4 | Reality Collapse | The east wing returns; hallways loop; memories become rooms | Ch. 3–4 |
| 5 | Final Truth | Why the loop exists; end it, control it, or stay | Ch. 5 |

## Characters

**Mira Okafor** — Class president, the player's seatmate, an organizer who cannot finish the Final Assembly speech (213 drafts, all dated today). *Secret (Ch. 3):* she is not aware of the loop, but her refusal to say goodbye is feeding it. The Stillwell keeps whatever someone cannot let go of, and Mira has been unable to let go of Halloway since her older brother graduated and never came back. Her route decides whether "Thursday Forever" is a happy ending or a trap.

**Theo Vance** — A/V kid and conspiracy theorist. Has partially noticed the loop for years: he leaves notes for himself (`—T.V.`) under the bleachers. In Phase 2 the school "files him away" and everyone forgets him. Rescuing him from the Lost & Found makes him permanently loop-aware and the comic heart of the party.

**June Sato** — Quiet artist who counts things to stay grounded. She draws the door to 2-F without knowing why, because she walked past it every day before the school painted over it. *Ch. 3:* her lost sketchbook is the key to mapping the east wing.

**Mr. Aldous Grey** — History teacher, loop-aware since 1977, the person who did the head count. Kind, exhausted, quietly heroic. He gives the player the Second Hand watch. Delivering Felix's message ("it wasn't your fault") is the emotional climax of Chapter One. *Ch. 5:* he can choose to become the new Keeper so others can leave.

**Ms. Celeste Marrow** — Chemistry teacher, member of the Bellwether. Keeps the loop because inside it her cat Pip (died 2015) is waiting at home every night. Antagonist turned ally if you face her grief honestly.

**Coach Ray Dunmore** — Tells one joke forever. Completely unaware, except that every new event gives him a headache "like the building flinching." *Ch. 4:* his headaches become a mechanic: a living anomaly detector. His whistle in the Lost & Found is engraved 1977.

**Mrs. Ines Vell** — The librarian. Not human: she is what the library became after reading itself too many times. Neutral, gentle, archival. She opens the archive. *Ch. 5:* she can keep a record of everyone after the loop ends.

**Okon** — Custodian. Hums the bell slowed down. Holds the STILLWELL key and hears the spring breathe at night. Opens the way down.

**Principal Hask** — Leader of the Bellwether. Does not blink or breathe. *Truth (Ch. 4):* Hask is the Stillwell's hand, a shape it built to run the school. He believes he is protecting everyone.

**Felix Bram** — The boy from 1977, see-through, still waiting in 2-F for the head count and for "tomorrow's" eclipse. Wants to be an astronomer.

**The Stillwell** — Speaks in capitals. Not evil. Frightened. Has never been asked *why* it keeps things. Asking (Ch. 1 choice `well_why`) opens the Graduation route in Chapter 5.

**Students:** Priya Nair (debate captain, argues for Thursday), Dex Moreau (skater, has tried to kickflip over the fog), Ama Boateng (gossip who knows everything except what matters). Each gets a loop-specific mini-arc in later chapters.

## Factions

- **The Bellwether Society** — teachers who keep the loop on purpose. "No student ever fails. No one ever has to say goodbye." Minutes show one new member per decade.
- **The Truth Closet** — Theo's investigation club (Theo, the player, later June and Dex). A rival "student council investigation" led by Mira can form in Ch. 2, depending on whether you told Mira the truth.

## Routes and endings

Choices are tracked with permanent flags. The final branch (Ch. 5) depends on three axes:

1. **Who you freed:** Theo, Felix, Pip's memory, Grey's guilt, June's sketchbook.
2. **Who you told:** telling Mira, Theo or Marrow about the loop changes how they act in later loops.
3. **How you treated the Stillwell:** asked why (`well_why`), threatened it, or ignored it.

| Ending | How | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect Attendance | Reach loop 7 without making a single deduction | Quiet horror |
| Thursday Forever | Phase 2+, Mira bond 3, on the roof after 9 PM, choose to stay | Bittersweet |
| Early Dismissal | Phase 2+, have the chalkboard warning, throw the manual bell switch at 11:55+ | Dark |
| Tomorrow *(Ch. 5)* | End the loop; the school closes, everyone scatters, life goes on | Hopeful, painful |
| Keeper of Thursday *(Ch. 5)* | Take the Stillwell's place to let everyone else leave | Sacrificial |
| Graduation *(Ch. 5, true ending)* | Free everyone it kept, ask the Stillwell why, and let it rest | Cathartic |
| Eclipse *(secret)* | Felix finally sees the eclipse on October 14 | Joyful secret |

## Chapter One beat sheet

1. Wake in 2-B. Mira's greeting. Explore. (Phase 1)
2. Clues: Vell's "welcome back", the scratched desk, June's door, the missing room number, the calendar.
3. Loop 2: Mira repeats herself, Coach repeats his joke, the chalkboard warning in your handwriting.
4. **Deduction: The Day Repeats** → Phase 2.
5. Mr. Grey confides and gives the Second Hand.
6. **Deduction: There Was a Room 2-F** → meet Felix.
7. Loop 3+: Theo disappears. **Deduction: The Forgotten Are Kept** → rescue Theo from the Lost & Found.
8. Vell opens the archive: yearbook, closures, Bellwether minutes, blueprints.
9. Eavesdrop on the Bellwether meeting (office, 8–10 PM).
10. Deliver Felix's message to Grey (side story, achievement "Head Count").
11. **Deduction: The School Refuses to Close** → Phase 3. Okon opens the basement.
12. The Stillwell. End of Chapter One.

## Tone rules

- Every serious scene gets a small human joke near it, and every joke scene hides one sad detail.
- Characters never explain the mystery outright; the player assembles it on the board.
- Repetition is the medium: reuse exact lines on purpose so changes land hard.
